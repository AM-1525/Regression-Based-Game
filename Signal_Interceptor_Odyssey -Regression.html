SECURITY GATE

  • Goal: clear all 20 levels across 5 stages. Each stage teaches one data science idea.
  • Play: move the sliders, watch the live numbers and chart, then press LOCK IN. Nothing is explained up front: you discover the idea by experimenting.
  • Shields: 3. A wrong lock-in, a wrong riddle or a failed attempt costs one. Zero shields = mission over.
  • Hints: only 2 for the whole game. Use them wisely.
  • Mazes (stage 5): arrow keys / WASD or the on-screen pad. Amber gates are hands-on data puzzles you solve by experimenting; skull cells are noise traps that cost points.
  • Score: 100 per level + streak bonus. Your best score is saved on this device.
Learning objectives
  1. Stage 1: explain least squares: the best line minimises squared residuals (MSE) and outliers pull it.
  2. Stage 2: distinguish underfitting from overfitting using train vs unseen test error; use regularisation (λ).
  3. Stage 3: read precision and recall and see why moving a threshold trades one for the other.
  4. Stage 4: run k-means and pick k with the elbow method.
  5. Stage 5: experiment your way to a confounder, an outlier-resistant statistic and sampling bias.

All data is generated randomly each attempt; models are fitted live in your browser (normal equations, k-means, confusion matrix). Nothing is hard-coded.
